# Accepted Payment Methods

## Standard Payment Options
- Credit / Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express).
- Net 30 Terms available for pre-approved corporate and institutional accounts with minimum annual spend of $5,000.
- Wire Transfers / EFT for high-value export orders.

## Security & Card Storage
All transactions are processed through tokenized gateways. Card numbers are never retained in plain text on production web frontends.
