# Customer Privacy & Record Retention

## Information Collected
Ohmworks collects contact names, shipping addresses, email communications, and order histories to fulfill procurement contracts and provide warranty service.

## Data Retention
- Transaction histories are retained for tax and accounting compliance for a minimum of 7 years.
- Customer support ticket transcripts and automated assistant dialogues are archived for quality assurance.
- Personal data is never sold or shared with external advertising brokerages.
