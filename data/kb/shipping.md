# Shipping Rates and Logistics

## Domestic Shipping
- Standard Ground: 3-5 business days ($5.95 flat rate, free for orders over $75).
- Priority Air: 2 business days ($14.50 flat rate).
- Next-Day Critical: Next business morning ($28.00 flat rate for orders placed before 3:00 PM EST).

## International Shipping
- Standard Global: 7-14 business days. Customs duties and import VAT are the responsibility of the purchaser.
- Dangerous Goods: Lithium cells, capacitors exceeding 20Wh, and volatile flux solvents must travel via surface freight only.
