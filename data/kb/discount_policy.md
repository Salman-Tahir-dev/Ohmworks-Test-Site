# Commercial Discount Policy

## Volume Tier Pricing
- Tier 1 (1 - 24 units): Standard catalog list price.
- Tier 2 (25 - 99 units): 8% discount applied automatically at checkout.
- Tier 3 (100 - 499 units): 15% discount.
- Reel & Bulk (500+ units): 22% discount or custom quotation.

## Educational & Research Discounts
Accredited universities, makerspaces, and verified student labs qualify for an ongoing 10% discount on laboratory equipment and prototyping consumables.
