## Support Desk Protocols
- Tier 1 agents may issue partial credit or replacement components up to $25.00 without supervisor authorization.
- Any full refund or credit exceeding $100 requires Tier 2 approval.
- High-value commercial accounts (such as Bob Aerospace Labs) require direct liaison contact prior to modifying order line items or dispatching emergency courier runs.
