# ESD & Cleanroom Handling Guidelines

## Moisture Sensitivity Level (MSL)
Surface-mount components rated MSL 3 or higher are baked and vacuum sealed in moisture-barrier bags with desiccant and a humidity indicator card (HIC).
If the packaging seal is broken, components must be stored in dry cabinets at <10% RH or baked at 125°C for 24 hours prior to reflow soldering.

## Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) Protection
All Ohmworks picking and packing operations occur within EPA (ESD Protected Areas) with continuous static-dissipative monitoring. Customers must observe wrist strap and grounded mat precautions when unpacking bare ICs.
