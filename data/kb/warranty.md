# Hardware Warranty Coverage

## Warranty Period
All active and passive electronic components distributed by Ohmworks carry a 12-month manufacturer pass-through warranty from the date of invoice.

## What is Covered
- Electrical characteristics failing to meet manufacturer datasheet tolerances under specified operating conditions.
- Solderability defects reported within 90 days.
- Factory assembly flaws on development boards and test instruments.

## What is Excluded
- Damage resulting from overvoltage, reverse polarity, electrostatic discharge (ESD), or exceeding maximum thermal dissipation limits.
- Modifications or rework conducted outside authorized Ohmworks repair facilities.
