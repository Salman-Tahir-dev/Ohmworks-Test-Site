# Ohmworks Return & RMA Policy

## Standard Return Window
Components and hardware may be returned within 30 days of shipment receipt, subject to standard inspection.

## Conditions for Return
- Items must be in original anti-static packaging or unopened reel/tube.
- Cut tape components or soldered ICs cannot be returned unless verified defective prior to placement.
- Custom-cut leads or programmed microcontrollers are non-refundable.

## RMA Procedure
1. Contact customer support or submit a return request online.
2. An RMA number will be assigned within 24 business hours.
3. Affix the RMA label to the exterior shipping carton. Packages arriving without an RMA number will be rejected at the receiving dock.
